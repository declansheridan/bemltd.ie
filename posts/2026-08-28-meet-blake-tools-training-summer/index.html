Meet Blake 👷‍♂️⚽️

If you’ve seen us on site this summer, you’ve probably seen Blake alongside us—bringing a positive attitude on site and plenty of upbeat humour back in the office.

When he’s not captaining Crumlin United’s U17s, captaining the Leinster U17s team, or playing with Crumlin’s Senior 1 squad, Blake’s all in on learning about electric motors, fans, and pumps.

This summer he’s shown real drive and has already mastered:

  • Motor testing
  • Changing motor bearings
  • Replacing a mechanical seal

Blake’s got big ambitions in football, but he’s also serious about building a trade. To support that goal (and to say thanks for all the hard work), we gifted him his very own fully equipped Wiha tool bag and a Fluke meter.

Thanks, Blake—and best of luck with your studies and on the pitch.
